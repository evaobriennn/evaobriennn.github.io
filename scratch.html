My Favorite Places

Utah is home to some of the most breathtaking landscapes in the country. Here are a few of my favorites:

  1. Zion National Park
    • Angels Landing – a bucket list hike
    • The Narrows – hiking through the Virgin River
    • Emerald Pools Trail – great for all skill levels
  2. Bryce Canyon National Park
    • Sunrise Point – stunning at dawn
    • Queens Garden Trail – walk among the hoodoos
    • Stargazing – one of the darkest skies in the US
  3. Mount Nebo
    • Highest peak in the Wasatch Range
    • Payson Lakes – perfect for a summer day
    • Wildflowers in late July are incredible
  4. Arches National Park
    • Delicate Arch – iconic Utah landmark
    • Landscape Arch – longest natural arch in North America
    • Devil's Garden Trail – a full day adventure

Trail Tips

🥾 Gear Up

Wear sturdy hiking boots with ankle support. Utah trails can be rocky and uneven.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Bring more water than you think you need. The dry desert air dehydrates you faster than you expect.

🌅 Go Early

Start hikes before 8am to beat the heat and the crowds, especially in summer.

🗺️ Download Maps

Cell service is spotty in most parks. Download offline maps on AllTrails before you go.
